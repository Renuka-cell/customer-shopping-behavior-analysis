# 📊 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes customer shopping behavior using **Python, PostgreSQL/SQL, and Power BI**.

The project follows a practical analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → Python Data Cleaning → PostgreSQL → SQL Business Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using a dataset containing **3,900 customer purchase records**.

The objective is to understand customer spending patterns, product preferences, discount usage, subscription behavior, customer segments, shipping preferences, and revenue contribution across different age groups.

The project demonstrates how raw transactional data can be transformed into meaningful business insights using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

> **Project Note:** This project was developed as a guided learning project based on a publicly available tutorial workflow. I worked through the Python data preparation, PostgreSQL/SQL analysis, and Power BI dashboard implementation and documented the work for my portfolio.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation

Python and Pandas were used to prepare the raw dataset for further analysis.

### Data preparation steps

1. Loaded the CSV dataset using Pandas.
2. Examined the dataset structure using `info()` and `describe()`.
3. Checked for missing values.
4. Identified **37 missing values** in the `Review Rating` column.
5. Filled missing review ratings using the **median review rating within each product category**.
6. Standardized column names using `snake_case`.
7. Created an `age_group` feature by grouping customers based on age.
8. Created `purchase_frequency_days` by converting purchase-frequency categories into approximate day intervals.
9. Checked whether `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` contained redundant information.
10. Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column.
11. Connected the prepared DataFrame to PostgreSQL.
12. Loaded the cleaned dataset into PostgreSQL for SQL analysis.

After transformation, the working dataset contains **19 columns**.

# 🔎 SQL Business Analysis

The project contains 10 business-oriented SQL questions.

### 1. Revenue by Gender

**Question:**

> What is the total revenue generated by male vs. female customers?

The query groups customers by gender and calculates total purchase revenue.

---

### 2. High-Spending Discount Users

**Question:**

> Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?

The analysis identifies customers who used discounts while their purchase amount was at or above the overall average purchase amount.

---

### 3. Top 5 Products by Rating

**Question:**

> Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review rating?

Products are grouped and ranked according to their average review rating.

---

### 4. Shipping Type Comparison

**Question:**

> How do average purchase amounts compare between Standard and Express shipping?

The analysis compares the average purchase amount for Standard and Express shipping.

---

### 5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers

**Question:**

> Do subscribed customers spend more?

The analysis compares:

- Number of customers
- Average spending
- Total revenue

between subscribers and non-subscribers.

---

### 6. Discount-Dependent Products

**Question:**

> Which 5 products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts applied?

The percentage of discounted purchases is calculated for each product and the top five are identified.

---

### 7. Customer Segmentation

Customers are classified into three segments based on previous purchases:

- **New**
- **Returning**
- **Loyal**

This provides a simple view of customer purchase history and loyalty.

---

### 8. Top 3 Products per Category

**Question:**

> What are the top 3 most purchased products within each category?

Window functions and ranking are used to identify the most purchased products within each category.

---

### 9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions

**Question:**

> Are customers with more than 5 previous purchases also likely to subscribe?

Customers with more than five previous purchases are analyzed according to their subscription status.

---

### 10. Revenue by Age Group

**Question:**

> What is the revenue contribution of each age group?

Revenue is aggregated across the different customer age groups.
